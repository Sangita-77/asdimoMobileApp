Facebook User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 16, 2026

AsDimo integrates Facebook Login to allow swift, secure authentication. In compliance with Facebook Platform policies and data privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA), users have the right to request deletion of their data and remove AsDimo access at any time.

Option 1: Remove AsDimo via Facebook Settings

To revoke permissions and delete your Facebook login link with AsDimo:

  1. Log into your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites to view all third-party applications linked to your profile.
  3. Find and select AsDimo.
  4. Click the Remove button.
  5. To request full data deletion from AsDimo servers automatically, click View Removed Apps and Websites, select AsDimo, and click Send Request.

Option 2: Direct Data Purge Request

If you would like us to completely delete your AsDimo account, child profiles, and all associated database records:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to support@asdimo.com with the subject: Delete My Data - Facebook Login.
  2. Include your name and registered email ID or Facebook profile identifier.

Our support team will process the permanent deletion within 3 business days and confirm via email.
