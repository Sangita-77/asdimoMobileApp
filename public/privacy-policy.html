AsDimo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Welcome to AsDimo (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We are dedicated to safeguarding your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AsDimo collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data when you use our mobile application and related online services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, authenticate, or use AsDimo, we may collect:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, contact numbers, and login credentials.
  • Facebook / Google Social Authentication Data: If you use Facebook Login or Google Sign-In, we receive your public profile info (name, profile picture) and email address based on permissions granted by you.
  • Child & Assessment Information: Details voluntarily entered by parents regarding developmental assessments, milestones, and therapist bookings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Authenticate user accounts and facilitate secure sign-in/sign-up.
  • Provide developmental tools, interactive learning activities, and therapist appointment scheduling.
  • Send critical account, security, and verification notifications.
  • Maintain system security and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third-party advertisers. Information is only shared with authorized healthcare/therapy professionals you choose to consult with, and trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud database and hosting providers) strictly necessary to run the service.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement enterprise-grade encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest to secure your personal data against unauthorized disclosure or loss. Data is retained only as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services.

5. User Data Deletion & Facebook Data Removal

You can delete your account or request the removal of your Facebook-associated data at any time. For step-by-step instructions on removing the app from your Facebook account or submitting a data deletion request, please view our User Data Deletion Instructions or email us.
